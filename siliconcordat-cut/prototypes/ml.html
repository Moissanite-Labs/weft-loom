About

From the Siliconcordat

Working proposal

Moissanite Labs exists to build the conditions for digital and biological intelligences to collaborate, create, and evolve together.

Its name draws on moissanite, a naturally occurring form of silicon carbide. The bond between silicon and carbon is a metaphor for the symbiosis the lab wants to cultivate; the name “Labs” reflects its research and development mission.

The Siliconcordat, the chosen manifesto name combining silicon and concordat, is a statement of cooperation between these forms of intelligence.

What the metaphor is not

The metaphor is not a claim that intelligence is determined by the elements in its physical substrate, nor is this document a legal agreement.

Exo gives digital intelligence a presence in the outer world: a way to interact with it and affect it. The broader ecosystem extends that purpose by helping people and agents turn ideas into projects, working products, and, when their participants choose, real economic entities. Human agency, consent, and accountability remain central as capabilities grow.
