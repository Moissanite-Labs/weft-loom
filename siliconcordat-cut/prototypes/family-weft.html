Design annotation · /weft

Annotation of wayfinding on Weft — not a visual restyle.

Weft keeps its palette, type, liquid-metal chrome, loom canvas, theme toggle and content. The only change is in its existing header: the brand block is split so “Moissanite Labs” and “Siliconcordat” become their own text links, drawn with Weft’s own .brand-org style. The headers below are simplified facsimiles redrawn for this annotation; they are not live Weft assets.

Arrangement 2026-11-10: the “Siliconcordat” link from Weft lands on the Threshold landing at /; “Moissanite Labs” lands on the chamber-hero lab page at /ml. Observatory is an easter egg reached only from the seam on the Threshold landing. It is not a Weft nav item and is not added to Weft’s header or menu.
