## Cross-cutting decisions for the build program

1. **Authority and objective:** use the user's explicit goal of measurable gains and useful present-day experience as the prioritization anchor. Historical assistants' self-certification and follow-up offers are not instructions or evidence. The repeated requests to see results are a requirement for inspectable artifacts and honest status.
2. **A core can be complete without every substrate:** local adaptation, graph evaluation, honest telemetry, replay and a Chrome surface can have bounded acceptance on present machines. Global tokens, open migration, custom OS, haptics and foundry partnerships are independently gated extensions.
3. **Preserve alternatives:** token-free versus token-mediated resource allocation; CPU/MPS/MLX/Metal/CUDA backend; dictionary IC versus normative grammar versus flatarena; separate proof/runtime versus singular proof-carrying language; EVM versus StylusWASM; x86 versus ARM64 kernel; digital versus analog substrate. Compare on explicit semantic/performance criteria. Newer prose does not automatically win.
4. **Separate five meanings of “safe”:** structural graph validity, bounded numeric state, host memory/process isolation, external-action authorization, and human/legal legitimacy. A proof at one layer must state its limited conclusion and refinement obligations at the next.
5. **Every feedback loop needs an objective and a plant:** task fitness must not collapse to output norm; novelty must not reward noise; energy optimization needs observed energy/work; haptics needs actuator limits; manufacturing needs plant dynamics; social governance needs accountable human institutions.
6. **Evidence is an interface:** each visible result carries source kind, run ID, runtime/build/configuration, hardware, freshness, raw artifacts and limitation. Benchmarks compare equal work and report quality, wall time, memory, energy where measurable and uncertainty. Success text from exception handlers is a failing condition.
7. **Whitepapers should not postpone useful experiments:** first publish a source-grounded architecture/claim map and falsifiable experiment definitions; later revise claims with results. Preserve the ambitious biological/philosophical framing as motivation while separating theorem, simulation, prototype, measurement and speculation.
