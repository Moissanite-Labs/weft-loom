## 2. Why colonies satisfice instead of compounding

Across Weft Paper 1, Paper 2, `E07`, and `razi-cells-laws-2026-11`, three bottlenecks explain why multi-cell and multi-generation runs hit a ceiling early.

**1. Free rest makes coasting the rational optimum (`K18`, `K20`, `L6`).**
In `code/metab.py`, a newborn heir in `L1` receives an already-assayed passing body. It sees that a `mid` step costs 50 to 100 energy while a +10% gain pays 38, and that calling `rest` returns all unspent energy to the lineage store safely.

Under those laws, stopping immediately is rational. In `L1`, 65 of 70 heirs did zero writes or variant rounds (`D2`), even though 42 of those 65 read a sense number below `0.90` of the record. Yet when an heir *did* attempt a change (`4` lineage-generations), the germline gate accepted `3` of the `4` (`+0.97`, `+0.13`, `+0.27`, `D5`). The gate is not blocking progress. The birth law lets heirs coast.

**2. Symmetric code inheritance anchors the search basin (`K1`, `L7`).**
When the best solution requires a discrete algorithmic jump rather than local tuning (such as switching `compress` from `zlib` to `lzma` framing), blank restarts (`arm b`) found the ceiling body in `2/3` runs (`1.542`) while heredity (`arm h`) found it in `0/3` (`1.20`).

Putting the parent's full code in the heir's working directory anchors the model to local edits around generation 0. Biology solves this with asymmetric division: one daughter preserves the germline state, and the other is forced to differentiate or explore.

**3. The assay tasks (`run/needs4k.json`) are one-life, single-file, wall-clock timed puzzles.**
`compress` and `csvsum` reach near-ceiling on the first stdlib body, and `fibmod` can jump from `1.0x` to `5.37x` in a single `mid` body (`msmoke1`). Because one life can finish the task, 8 independent blank draws (`arm b`) tie 4 generations of inheritance (`arm h`).

Furthermore, because `perf_counter` on a single machine is noisy and vulnerable to thermal carryover (`G6`), every assay acquires a machine-wide `.bench.lock` (`E07/razi-cells-2026-11/convergence.md`). That turns an 8-cell parallel colony into a serial queue and penalizes wider colonies (`g4` dropped to `1.57` wins/100 calls vs `g3` `3.12`).

## 3. Next moves for the team (ranked by information per run)

### Move 1: Run `T-L2` (asymmetric division at birth) with a `W0` baseline check (`W2`)
In `code/lineage.py`, make each generation's `width=2` birth asymmetric:
- **Daughter 0 (Conservative)**: Inherits the germline body (`fibmod.py`) and cannot rest until it runs at least one `variants` round or hand rewrite (`hold` on the heir).
- **Daughter 1 (Exploratory)**: Born blank (no code file, breaking the `L7` anchoring trap), receives the lineage `LIBRARY` lessons and the `sense` ceiling ratio, and makes an independent draw.
- **Gate**: Both daughters compete against the held germline body on 5 interleaved repeats, plus a mandatory differential check against generation-0 reference outputs (`W2`, matching Paper 1 Proposition 3's anchored ball invariant around $W_0$).

This uses the existing `run/needs4k.json` and `code/lineage.py` harness with ~30 lines of code. It tests whether combining `arm h` survival with `arm b` basin exploration beats both (`L8`), and addresses `L6` (heirs coasting) and `L7` (anchoring) in one experiment.

### Move 2: Close the two accounting leaks in `code/metab.py` before `T-L2` runs (`L9` and `K18`/`K24`)
- **Hard output cap (`L9`)**: `affordable()` currently checks estimated output tokens before the call, which let 1 call in `L1` (`l1b2` `compress`) overdraw (`208.2` spent on `175.4` balance). Cap the API `max_tokens` parameter on every call to `min(CELL_MAX_OUT, max_affordable_out_tokens(energy))` so overdrafts cannot happen.
- **Price-to-reward calibration (`K18`/`K24`)**: Across 22 energy-law runs (`751` steps), `heavy` was chosen `0` times and `14/16` cells dropped to `light` immediately, even though `SP3` showed `mid` writes near-record first bodies on `fibmod` (`4/10` vs `0/6` on `light`).
  Paying reward proportional to *share of remaining gap closed* (using the `SP3` sense ratio) or applying the `GR4` escalation law (after a failed `light` round, discount or require one `mid` step before resting) aligns local cost with colony reach.

### Move 3: Upgrade the assay instrument for `T-L3` (`G1` / `R2` / `J2`)
Emergent specialization (`J2`, stem niches `J4a`) cannot appear when one Python function in one file is the entire organism.
- **Replace wall-clock `perf_counter` with a deterministic work counter** so `.bench.lock` can be removed and 8 to 32 cells can assay in parallel with zero noise:
  - *Option A (Recommended default: Python VM op-count + output size)*: Count exact Python VM opcodes (`sys.monitoring`) or instruction counts and output bytes on fixed inputs. Zero OS timing jitter and zero language-training-data confound.
  - *Option B (Lean 4 theorem needs, `R2`)*: Lean `4.35.0` is installed (`~/.elan/bin/lake`). Needs are theorem files with `sorry` stubs, and fitness is the count of proved lemmas (`lake build` passes with zero noise).
  - *Option C (Bend / HVM interaction count, `G1`)*: `bend run-c -s` gives deterministic `ITRS` counts (`6,029,296` across 3/3 runs), connecting directly to Weft Paper 2. Run a 1-task smoke test first to measure how reliably frontier models write valid Bend syntax.
- **Make the need compositional and ratcheting (`T-L3`)**: Give a colony a multi-stage pipeline or a stepped test suite where passing tier $k$ unlocks tier $k+1$ with higher energy payouts. A single cell in one life can clear tier 1 or 2, while clearing tier 4 requires heirs to inherit working modules and specialize.
