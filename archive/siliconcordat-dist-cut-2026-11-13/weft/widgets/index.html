Early sketches

Five widgets from the original sessions

These are kept as they were exported from the design conversation that started Weft. They are illustrations, not implementations. Each title says what the code actually does, and the filenames keep their historical names. The exports load fonts and a stylesheet from Google, so they need a network connection.

Source: weft-corpus/weft/browser-sources/widgets
