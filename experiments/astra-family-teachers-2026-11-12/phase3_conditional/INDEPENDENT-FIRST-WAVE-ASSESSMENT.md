# Independent first-wave assessment and follow-up recommendation

Reviewed the completed first-wave results and summary. This assessment was written after those outcomes were visible. any follow-up is a new experiment.

The real-weight-plus-hidden condition reduces IID active movement MSE by 19.11%, 21.86%, and 20.77% for seeds 17, 29, and 43. Only seeds 17 and 43 meet retention, satisfying the registered two-of-three exploratory source criterion. Seed 29 low-urgency drift is 0.0005211 against a 0.0005 budget.

All three fail shifted retention, despite shifted active improvements around 20%: unchanged-context drift rises to 0.000700–0.000812. This is a real robustness limitation, not a failed active-target improvement.

Behavior-only selection produces IID changes of -0.37%, -0.35%, and +0.59% improvement. The real representation helps on these cases, but that representation includes both weights and hidden activations, while behavior-only removes both. The result does not isolate numerical-weight information.

The scrambled condition is especially informative: seed 43 achieves 14.32% IID improvement and 13.77% shifted improvement, with retention on both. Its proposal acts on movement readout units 9 and 5, which also appear in the true-weight first proposal.

Because the scrambled packet retains truthful hidden activations, hidden-unit identity and directional features can be inferred despite false matrices. This is an alternative route to informed editing, not proof of generic blind attenuation.

The real-weight first proposal still has lower IID active MSE than scrambled on all three seeds, but three pairs do not establish a population result.

Known alignment transfers active improvements consistently: sibling family means are 18.88%, 21.66%, and 20.39%. raw-coordinate means are 4.31%, 2.69%, and -0.25%. Retention passes 4/4, 0/4, and 4/4 aligned siblings on IID and 0/12 on shift. This demonstrates value of provided correspondence, not discovery of correspondence.

Closed-loop collisions summed across three rollout seeds decline from 2,283 to 2,116. 2,587 to 2,108. and 2,742 to 2,262 for aligned family edits. Raw transfer has worse imitation fidelity but even fewer collisions for family 43 (1,969). Collision reduction and static target fidelity are distinct outcomes. neither should substitute for the other.

The existing hazard encounter counts are sparse, limiting ecological safety claims.

## Recommended next information

Run a fresh-seed 2×2 representation ablation: numerical weights present/absent crossed with truthful hidden activations present/absent. Keep the same examples and target, one independent proposal per condition, and all four conditions on each fresh source. This directly resolves the largest current attribution ambiguity without repeating calibration.

Twelve calls on three sources should cost materially less than the available budget. there is no scientific need to exhaust expiring credits.

A complementary direct-colony arm can supply training-rollout feedback and select for collision reduction subject to goal-progress, action-energy, hazard and static retention constraints. Use fresh held-out rollout seeds and report all outcomes, including no eligible patch.

That would test whether the teacher can optimize a colony objective rather than rely on the imitation objective's incidental ecological effects. It must be reported as a distinct objective with a distinct information budget.

Tool-assisted editing and pretrained-language-model repair are potentially valuable, but introduce more moving parts and attribution problems within a short implementation window. The representation factorial is the most direct new information from the observed first-wave surprise.
