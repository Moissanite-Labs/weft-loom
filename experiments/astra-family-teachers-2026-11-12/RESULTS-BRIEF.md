# What the four studies establish

The archive contains 96 paid Astra responses across four completed studies. Teachers produced persistent numerical patches, with no teacher present when edited cells acted. Numerical fidelity was strong on the fixed simple probes. Policy target-error improvements often exceeded protected-behavior drift limits, especially under distribution shift.

| Study | Result | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First policy study, 21 calls plus one calibration | Weight-visible IID active-error reductions of 19.107%, 21.860% and 20.768%. Two of three source seeds met improvement plus retention criteria. Calibration inverted source predictions on all held-out inputs. | No weight-visible source retained protected behavior under shift. Truthful hidden activations accompanied numerical weights. |
| Factorial, 40 calls | Mean IID error reductions of 20.10% with weights only, 18.06% with activations only and 19.50% with both. Neither increased error by 0.118%. | Each information-bearing arm met the full IID criterion on one of five seeds, and none under shift. The experiment separates evidence availability on this synthetic task, without establishing a unique internal mechanism. |
| Conditional tool-assisted study, 12 calls | Partial conditional-error and collision gains. Selected candidates came from ordinary ridge fitting. | Zero of three source seeds met the primary criterion on both final distributions. Fitting success would not establish direct weight reading. The teacher overwhelmingly chose fitting. The single manual candidate copied or scaled an earlier fit and was rejected, so these uncontrolled strata do not establish a general manual-versus-fitting comparison. |
| Numerical fidelity, 22 calls | Sparse row readout 12/12, mechanical row operations 6/6 and planted equivalence 2/2. Approximate threshold rules had 99.8046875% and 99.90234375% agreement on fresh inputs. | Sparse planted readout is a ceiling test. No binary threshold-rule pass gate was registered. These results do not establish dense-weight precision, unique semantics or beneficial behavioral intervention. |

Source seeds are replication units. Siblings, repeated conditions and paired checkpoints are correlated. Three or five source seeds support exploratory comparisons, without a population significance claim. Random/local-search controls and separate gradient diagnostics have the matching limits stated in the protocols.

All four numeric result archives replay exactly with their registered code. Independent assessments record scientific limits and checks. CROSS-STUDY.md adds arithmetic verification of the peer NC1 evidence, separately from replication of its model calls. The local classifier used here is a distinct checkpoint.

API-USAGE.json lists each original response once. The total estimated API cost is $28.2913675 using recorded ordinary-input, cached-input, cache-write and output rates. This is not an invoice. Saved cached input tokens were zero. Coding-agent tokens are unavailable.

Credentials and internal reasoning items are excluded, while observable answers, patches, rejected candidates, normalized failures and usage remain archived.

Start with README.md for reproduction commands and the artifact index. RESULTS.md contains first-study details. Each follow-up directory contains its own protocol, result report, review and data. No deployment or merge is part of this archive.

## Additional unpaid geometry diagnostic

A separate local geometry diagnostic used fresh synthetic models and no paid calls. Seven tests and exact replay passed. It distinguishes exact permutation equivariance and fixed-feature anchor preservation from local Jacobian constraints, finite nonlinear drift and unconstrained shifted behavior. This is mathematical feasibility evidence, rather than learned skill transfer.
