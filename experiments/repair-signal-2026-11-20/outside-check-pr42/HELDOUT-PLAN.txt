## Definitions (applied identically to every tier and model)

Per tier, per instance i, per arm a, the round records sorted by `round`.

- unreadable(round): `parse_error` is not null.
- pass4(a,i): some round has `verdict == "pass"`.
- esc(a,i): some round is unreadable.
- pure(a,i): pass4 and not esc.
- r1fail(i): round 1 has `verdict == "fail"` (an unreadable round 1 is not counted, as in their code).
- r2esc(a,i): r1fail, a round 2 exists, and round 2 is unreadable.
- r2pass(a,i): r1fail and round 2 has `verdict == "pass"`.
- solve_time(a,i): the sum of `seconds` over rounds 1 to the passing round.
- tot_time(a,i): the sum of `seconds` over all rounds of the unit.
- T*(tier): 10 times the median of `seconds` over all readable rounds of the tier, all arms pooled, median taken as the element at index floor(n/2) of the ascending list. (On Qwen S this gives about 30 s, the window their code uses.)
- Pairing: for arms X and Y, instance i is dropped from an X-vs-Y test if the round-1 `reply_sha256` differs between X and Y. The number dropped is reported.
- Sign test: exact one-sided binomial on discordant pairs, p = sum over k >= w of C(n,k) / 2^n, with w the count in the claimed direction and n the discordant total. Computed in integers.

## Tests

Primary family (8 tests, Holm step-down over all 8 at family alpha 0.05, one-sided in the direction PR 42 claims):

| id | per tier | statistic | claimed direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | S, M | paired r2esc, D-only vs C-only | D > C |
| G2 | S, M | paired pure, C-only vs D-only | C > D |
| G3 | S, M | paired r2pass, C-only vs D-only | C > D |
| G4 | S, M | paired pass4, D-only vs C-only (not blind) | D > C |

Decision per claim: "replicates on gemma" if at least one tier has Holm-adjusted p <= 0.05 in the claimed direction. "Contradicted" if at either tier the one-sided p in the opposite direction is <= 0.025 (unadjusted). Otherwise "not replicated".

Secondary (reported with raw p, alpha 0.025 one-sided, no family correction, no verdict hangs on them):

- G5 Simpson structure (not blind): Delta_direct = #pure(C) - #pure(D), Delta_total = #pass4(C) - #pass4(D). Present iff Delta_direct > 0 and Delta_total < 0.
- G6 Compute: sum of tot_time over instances, D over C. Paired sign test on tot_time per instance, D > C, ties dropped.
- G7 Windows: survival counts (solve_time <= T) for T in {5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 180, 300} s and T*; paired C > D sign test at T = 30 s and at T*.
- G8 Repaired fast-channel test (my strongest version of their claim, also run on Qwen): restricted to instances where neither C nor D had any unreadable round, paired pass4 C-only vs D-only, C > D.
- G9 What the extra unreadable replies are: each round-2 unreadable reply (r2esc) is classed "decimal-assignment" if, after removing one surrounding code fence, every non-empty line matches `^\s*x\d+\s*=\s*[-+]?\d+(\.\d+)?(/\d+)?\s*$` and at least one value has a decimal point; "assignment-other" if every line matches but none has a decimal point; else "prose". Report counts by arm and the median reply length in characters. Also B vs C paired r2esc (their `r2_cot_escape_B_vs_C`), B > C.

## Which claims cannot transfer, and why

1. "The flipped sign triggers long open-reasoning replies (about 80 s) that break the format." Gemma ran with `--reasoning on --reasoning-budget 1000` (PREREG.md, Models and settings); its reasoning is private and not sent back into the chat, and the README says its unreadable replies are mostly decimal answer lines. So the open-reasoning mechanism is absent by design. Only the generic form ("D causes more round-2 unreadable replies than C") is tested (G1), and G9 says what those replies are.
2. Time windows. Gemma's `seconds` include its private reasoning (up to 1000 tokens a round), so a short time does not mean "no side channel" and 30 s is not the same budget it was for Qwen. G7 is description only; the literal 30 s and the scaled T* are both reported.
3. Compute ratio. On gemma it measures hidden reasoning length per round and rounds used, not an open side channel. G6 is description.
4. "Simpson's paradox" and the D > C reversal (G4, G5) are not blind (see above).
