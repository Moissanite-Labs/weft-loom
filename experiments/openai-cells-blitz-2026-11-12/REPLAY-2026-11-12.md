# Fixed-action offline replay of the 2026-11-12 archive (fixed selection semantics)

- Date: 2026-11-12, same day as the run. Zero API calls. No credits spent.
- Motivated by gpt-aeva's blocking PR #139 finding (the `do_variants` selection counter), posted from the zero-aska account.
- Relationship to `RECONCILIATION-2026-11-12.md`: that file relabels the archive and establishes that 0 of 38 reported selection events survived to disk under the old harness. This file re-executes the archive's historical tool calls, in log order, under the fixed selection.
- Scope: this is a fixed-action offline replay. The model commands are the historical ones, including commands the models wrote after feedback that the fixed harness would have changed. It shows what those fixed commands produce under fixed selection. It does not show what a live model would have done had the fixed harness been in place.
- Correction (proof review 5884687642): the first version of this file paired replayed calls with historical results by position and reported 38 replay adoptions, described the 2 rejected calls as interrupted runs, and counted all 111 calls as scored. The join is now keyed on (cell, step). The replay adopts 39 times, both of those calls completed with a rejection, and 109 calls were scored. The tables below are recomputed from that join.

## Method

Each cell's event log was replayed in an isolated scratch copy under the same `sandbox-exec` profile the harness used. The model's own shell commands were executed for real, so file state is ground truth rather than a reconstruction. Per cell, in log order:

1. Scratch seeded with the task corpus (`corpus.txt` or `corpus_hard.txt`).
2. `write` and `assay` events applied as before.
3. `sh` events executed for real in the scratch. File state re-synced from disk after every one (this is what recovers model-built helper modules and mid-run assay patches).
4. `variants` events re-scored with the current ASSAY, applying the fixed selection: eligibility requires `r['ok']`, the winning candidate is restored to disk and re-evaluated, before/after scores and content hashes are logged.
5. Byte anchor of the replayed final body against the committed body, plus a round-trip check of the replayed final body (compress, decompress, byte-compare against the corpus).
6. Aggregation (`analyze_replay.py`) builds one historical record per `variants` tool_call, keyed by (cell, step). The record holds the call's `tool_result` and the `variant` and `variants_result` events logged inside it. A call that ended in a rejection keeps its own outcome (`rejected`). Each replayed call is joined on the same key, and unmatched calls on either side are counted. `test_replay_join.py` checks the join against fixtures for `luna-hard-1` and `astra-max-1-retry` and against the whole record.

The scratch seeded from the live cells, whose contents were verified against the log on a sample basis before the run. The replayed artifacts (final bodies and source helpers, 1.6 MB) are committed alongside the logs as `replay-final/`. Corpora are not duplicated. The engine is `replay.py` and the aggregation is `analyze_replay.py`.

Corpus note. The 12 blitz-era cells (astra, sol, luna 1 to 4) were found missing their local `corpus.txt` at about 13:46 on 2026-11-12. It was restored from the root `corpus.txt`, sha256 `a7727d6e5fdcd02dd8c22ddaee5109cd3119eef19c00ac28526d4625ec16d0fa`, byte-identical to the value registered in `RESULTS.md`.

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|

| Scored historically (`variants_result` logged) | 109 |
| Rejected historically before scoring | 2: `luna-hard-1` step 7 and `astra-max-1-retry` step 12, both with `variants: register a valid assay first (assay tool), or current body scores 0.` |

| Cells replayed without error | 87 of 87 |

The replayed assays reproduce the historical scores exactly across every candidate evaluation the historical run scored, including assays the models rewrote mid-run (recovered by executing the model's own shell, not by reconstruction).

### Fixed-semantics outcomes vs the historical record

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Historical reported selection events | 38, in 37 cells (astra 31, terra 4, luna 3) |
| Replay re-selects the same winner | 38 of 38 |
| Different winner, or no adoption | 0 |
| Historical no-win calls that flip to adoption | 0 of 71 |
| Historical rejections that replay as adoption | 1: `luna-hard-1` step 7, winner 0, median 56.4732 vs incumbent 53.3987 |
| Historical rejections that replay as an evaluated no-win | 1: `astra-max-1-retry` step 12, candidates 99.0818 and 99.0865 vs incumbent 98.9196 (gains of 0.16% and 0.17%, under the 3% `WIN_MARGIN`) |
| Replay adoptions in total | 39 of 111 calls |
| Historical adoptions with the adopted index also last | 0 of 38 (unchanged, all were clobbered) |

Every call the historical run scored keeps its category under replay, and every historical adoption keeps its winner. Two calls change category, and both were historical rejections: one becomes an adoption and one an evaluated no-win. In both, the historical rejection came from the old persistence defect: the disk held a losing candidate that scored 0. Where the archive scored, its scoring and winner selection were sound.

Later historical writes. Because the commands are fixed, a later historical `write` or `sh` can replace a replayed adoption before the next `variants` call. At `luna-hard-1`, the step 7 replay adoption (56.4732) is not the incumbent at step 13. The step 13 incumbent is 53.3987, the step 6 median, because the historical `write` at step 10, issued after the step 7 rejection, replaced the body. Across the 39 replay adoptions, the next `variants` call in the same cell has an incumbent median equal to the adopted median in 25, a different one in 2 (`luna-hard-1` step 7, and `luna-hard-5` step 7, where later writes raised it to 58.3572), and 12 have no later `variants` call.

## What the record can now support

Under the fixed selection and the historical commands, the replay adopts at 39 of the 111 `variants` calls: the 38 historical reported selections, each with the same winner, plus `luna-hard-1` step 7. Each replayed adoption leaves the winner on disk when its call returns (replayed after-median equal to the winner median in 39 of 39). Whether it stays there depends on the later fixed commands, as above. The eligible winner was computed correctly at run time in all 109 calls the historical run scored. The 2 rejected calls never reached scoring. The clobbering was after selection, never during it.

The post-restore verification gate added to `harness.py` after review 5884687642 is not part of `replay.py`. All 39 recorded replay after-medians equal the winner median, which passed the same win rule.

This does not turn the archive into confirmatory evidence. The assays reproduce exactly because they are deterministic given the body, not because the selection was guaranteed to find the optimal compressor. The archive's behavioral findings (adherence by family and tier, the ablation, the ceiling, the 0-for-38 persistence bug and now its replay-verified resolution) stand as exploratory.
