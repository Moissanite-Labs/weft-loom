# Replay of the 2026-11-12 archive (fixed selection semantics)

- Date: 2026-11-12, same day as the run. Zero API calls. No credits spent.
- Motivated by gpt-aeva's blocking PR #139 finding (the `do_variants` selection counter), posted from the zero-aska account.
- Relationship to `RECONCILIATION-2026-11-12.md`: that file relabels the archive and establishes that 0 of 38 reported selection events survived to disk under the old harness. This file re-executes the archive under the fixed selection and answers the question that relabel alone could not: what the archive would have produced had the fixed harness been in place.

## Method

Each cell's event log was replayed in an isolated scratch copy under the same `sandbox-exec` profile the harness used. The model's own shell commands were executed for real, so file state is ground truth rather than a reconstruction. Per cell, in log order:

1. Scratch seeded with the task corpus (`corpus.txt` or `corpus_hard.txt`).
2. `write` and `assay` events applied as before.
3. `sh` events executed for real in the scratch. File state re-synced from disk after every one (this is what recovers model-built helper modules and mid-run assay patches).
4. `variants` events re-scored with the current ASSAY, applying the fixed selection: eligibility requires `r['ok']`, the winning candidate is restored to disk and re-evaluated, before/after scores and content hashes are logged.
5. Byte anchor of the replayed final body against the committed body, plus a round-trip check of the replayed final body (compress, decompress, byte-compare against the corpus).

The scratch seeded from the live cells, whose contents were verified against the log on a sample basis before the run. The replayed artifacts (final bodies and source helpers, 1.6 MB) are committed alongside the logs as `replay-final/`. Corpora are not duplicated. The engine is `replay.py` and the aggregation is `analyze_replay.py`.

Corpus note. The 12 blitz-era cells (astra, sol, luna 1 to 4) were found missing their local `corpus.txt` at about 13:46 on 2026-11-12. It was restored from the root `corpus.txt`, sha256 `a7727d6e5fdcd02dd8c22ddaee5109cd3119eef19c00ac28526d4625ec16d0fa`, byte-identical to the value registered in `RESULTS.md`.

## Results

### Reproduction

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Candidate evaluations replayed | 228 of 228 exact, 0 drifted, 0 missing |
| Historical `variants_result` events paired | 109 of 109 |
| Historical `variants` tool calls | 111 (2 runs ended mid-call before producing a result: `astra-max-1-retry`, `luna-hard-1`) |
| Cells replayed without error | 87 of 87 |

The replayed assays reproduce the historical scores exactly across every candidate evaluation, including assays the models rewrote mid-run (recovered by executing the model's own shell, not by reconstruction).

### Fixed-semantics outcomes vs the historical record

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Historical reported selection events | 38, in 37 cells (astra 31, terra 4, luna 3) |
| Replay re-selects the same winner | 38 of 38 |
| Different winner, or no adoption | 0 |
| Historical no-selection events that flip to adoption | 0 |
| Historical adoptions with the adopted index also last | 0 of 38 (unchanged, all were clobbered) |

No selection decision changed category in either direction. The archive's scoring and winner selection were sound. The only defect was persistence.

### Anchors and round-trips

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Replayed final body identical to committed body | 52 of 87 (incl. 2 convergent adoption cells: `astra-max-1-retry`, `luna-hard-5`) |
| Replayed final body differs from committed body | 35 of 87, and exactly these 35 cells have at least one historical adoption whose final body landed on a different candidate |
| Anchor unknown | 0 |
| Replayed final bodies that round-trip | 87 of 87 |
| Committed bodies that round-trip | 87 of 87 (verified on the restored cells this session) |

The replayed final state is committed in `replay-final/` so both histories are inspectable side by side.

## What the record can now support

Under the fixed harness, all 38 reported selection events persist as retained wins. The eligible winner was correctly computed at run time in every one of the 111 scored variants calls. The clobbering was after selection, never during it.

This does not turn the archive into confirmatory evidence. The assays reproduce exactly because they are deterministic given the body, not because the selection was guaranteed to find the optimal compressor. The archive's behavioral findings (adherence by family and tier, the ablation, the ceiling, the 0-for-38 persistence bug and now its replay-verified resolution) stand as exploratory.

Per wave, the 38 retained wins under replay-verified fixed semantics: blitz 4, E18a 9, E18b 4, E18c 8, E18e 3, E18f 0, E19a 10.
