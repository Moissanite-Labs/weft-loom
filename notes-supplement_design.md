## Decisions and implications

1. Treat procedural rendering, behavioral feature capture, model inference, online learning and native adaptation as separately testable components. SDF animation does not require Laya or ledger settlement. Browser execution does not automatically gain POSIX shared memory or native MPS access.
2. Preserve the user’s explicit desire for positive reading feedback (alive.md:88), ordinary static hosting (anew.md:64), and an explanatory biological animation (bio.md:102). Assistant claims of calibration, cognition, fixed latency and production readiness are hypotheses requiring evidence.
3. Theme import/export and normalized aesthetic mixing are immediately useful independent deliverables. Preserve two-vector and many-vector versions; correct circular hue semantics, invalid weights and schema validation.
4. Organizational metrics require denominators, windows, provenance and unavailable states. The build.md harvest pipeline uses fixtures; a SHA-256 digest neither authenticates a publisher nor validates a metric.
5. Native/browser coupling and attention-linked economic settlement are later experiments with explicit interfaces and validity tests. They cannot inherit the confidence of a working shader.

4. **Model experiment:** pinned Laya checkpoint/runtime, dedicated worker, real API, warm/cold/download/memory/device measurements versus heuristic baseline. Exit: predefined quality/calibration and interaction performance targets; no cognitive claim from scroll alone.
5. **Intent/adaptation experiment:** bounded task mapping and offline A/B with undo; define task success independent of engagement duration. Separate fixed inference, preference learning and online weight updates.
6. **NCA research:** reproduce trained growth,persistence,damage metrics at small grid; port inference after parity. Semantic text/actions do not follow automatically from pixel recovery.
7. **Shared ecology:** consented profile publishing and influence rules; conflict/retry/moderation/auth design before shared writeback. Build with reviewable proposals; deploy only under explicit authorization.
8. **Native fusion/ledger:** typed local bridge and trace replay before adaptive cells; attention rewards require valid measurement and policy and stay optional downstream research.
